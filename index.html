Frequently asked questions

Mobile app growth, explained.

How acquisition, monetization, creative strategy and analytics work together for your mobile game or app.

How does Brighter Ads help mobile games and apps grow?

Brighter Ads is an independent mobile growth consultancy for game studios and app businesses, from startups to established teams. We combine user acquisition, monetization, creative strategy and data analytics, with strategic advice and hands-on support shaped around your product, budget and growth goals.

What does mobile user acquisition support include?

Mobile user acquisition support covers acquisition strategy, campaign setup, paid media management, performance optimization and scaling. Brighter Ads uses campaign results alongside user retention and lifetime value (LTV) to help teams identify high-value audiences and decide where to allocate budget for sustainable app growth.

What are CPI, CPE, CPA and ROAS in performance marketing?

CPI (cost per install) measures acquisition spend per app install. CPE (cost per engagement) measures spend per defined engagement event, while CPA (cost per action) measures spend per target action, such as a purchase or registration. ROAS (return on ad spend) compares attributed revenue with advertising spend over a defined period. Brighter Ads helps select campaign models and optimization goals around these metrics, considering retention and LTV alongside acquisition cost.

How can I improve app monetization and revenue?

Brighter Ads reviews in-app advertising (IAA), ad mediation, in-app purchases (IAP) and subscription strategy to identify revenue opportunities. The work can include ad placement and frequency, pricing optimization, subscription product development and A/B testing. We assess revenue changes alongside user engagement and retention to balance profitability with the user experience.

What do ARPDAU and LTV tell me about app growth?

ARPDAU (average revenue per daily active user) shows how much revenue your app generates per active user on a given day. LTV (lifetime value) estimates the revenue a user generates over their relationship with the app. Read alongside acquisition costs, ROAS and retention, these metrics help assess monetization changes and whether acquisition can scale profitably.

What goes into a creative strategy for mobile ads?

Brighter Ads develops creative concepts and testing plans using user behavior, audience insights and brand identity. This includes storytelling, user-generated content (UGC) concepts, video ad strategy and hook testing for different stages of the user journey. Continuous A/B testing and creative performance analysis guide iteration, identify promising concepts and help address creative fatigue before scaling.

How do data analytics and cohort analysis improve growth decisions?

Brighter Ads connects campaign reporting and performance dashboards with user segmentation, retention analysis, ROAS and LTV forecasting. Cohort analysis compares groups of users acquired in the same period or through the same channel to understand how their behavior and value change over time. These user insights help teams refine acquisition, monetization and product strategies, rather than judging a campaign only by its install volume.
